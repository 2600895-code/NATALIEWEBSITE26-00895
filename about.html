About Aroma Coffee and Tea House 🤎

Aroma Coffee and Tea House was established to provide customers with high-quality coffee and tea at affordable prices. The company was started to give people a comfortable place where they can enjoy their favourite drinks, relax, and spend time with friends and family.Aroma Coffee and tea house was established in Nairobi,kenya to provide customers with high quality coffee and tea at affordable prices .It was by Peter Chege in the year 2010.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide high-quality coffee and tea, excellent customer service, and affordable prices. We aim to create a welcoming environment where every customer feels comfortable and enjoys a refreshing experience.We are commited to satisfying our customers through quality products and welcoming services.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted and popular coffee and tea house known for quality products, friendly service, and customer satisfaction. We aim to grow our business and serve more customers in the future.And to be known for our high-quality products.We hope to expand our business and grow.
